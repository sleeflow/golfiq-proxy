Privacy Policy

Caddie Edge IQ — Last updated August 2026

Caddie Edge IQ ("the app") is built and operated by an independent developer, not a company. This page explains what information the app uses and why, in plain language.

Location Data

The app asks for your location one time, only to look up local wind speed and direction for your round using a weather service. Your location is used only for this purpose. It is not stored, not linked to your identity, and not shared with any third party beyond the weather lookup itself.

Golf Data You Enter

Your scores, club distances, handicap, and saved rounds are stored only on your own device. This information is not uploaded to any server, not sold, and not shared with anyone.

Caddie Advice Requests

When you request caddie advice, details about the current shot (distance, lie, wind, your club distances) are sent securely to the app's own server, which forwards the request to generate advice. These requests are used only to generate your advice in the moment and are not used to build a profile of you or sold to advertisers.

No Accounts, No Ads

The app does not require creating an account, does not show third-party ads, and does not use advertising trackers.

Children's Privacy

Caddie Edge IQ is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect information from children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.
